aggPings.csv contains the aggregated ping data from the ripe atlas platform from the 10th of October of 2022 to the 20th of October of 2022.
              The data has been curated by removing entries that do not contain geographical information, and by removing possible anycast addresses.

aggPingsClean.csv contains the same data from aggPings.csv but with the additional filter of removing entries that have the same geographical features with
                   a variability. Say for example, different nodes located in the same ASN, country, and coordinates, but with very different rtts.
